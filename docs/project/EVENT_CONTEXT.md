# Event Context — RESTART

## Concept

Your career is a journey. Every skill is a new ability. Every challenge is another mission. Every new decision can take you to another level.

## Career Journey (6 Levels)

1. **DISCOVER** — Explore career paths
2. **LEARN** — Expert sessions
3. **PRACTICE** — Workshops
4. **GET GUIDED** — 1-on-1 coaching
5. **CONNECT** — Networking
6. **LEVEL UP** — Next career step

## Fields (Configurable)

Programming & Software, AI, Data Analysis, Cybersecurity, Business & Entrepreneurship, Digital Marketing, Graphic Design, Freelancing

## Activities

Expert Sessions, Workshops, 1-on-1 Coaching, CV Reviews, Career Consulting, Interviews, Networking, Sponsor Booths
